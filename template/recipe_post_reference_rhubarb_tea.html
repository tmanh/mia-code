Easy rhubarb tea recipe with a refreshing tangy flavor. Made with simple ingredients, this fruity summer tea is delicious served hot or iced.

If you're looking for a simple and refreshing drink to enjoy on a warm day, this rhubarb tea recipe is worth trying. It combines the bright tanginess of rhubarb with the comforting aroma of tea, creating a drink that is both refreshing and satisfying.

This is one of my favorite tea recipes every summer. The tart flavor of rhubarb pairs beautifully with black tea, especially Earl Grey or lemon tea. The result is a lightly fruity, fragrant tea that helps wake me up in the morning and feels incredibly refreshing on hot days.

Whether you enjoy it warm or chilled over ice, this easy rhubarb tea is a wonderful way to use fresh rhubarb during the season.

Why You'll Love This Rhubarb Tea

This rhubarb tea is incredibly easy to make with just a handful of simple ingredients. It has a refreshing, naturally tangy flavor that makes it perfect for warm summer days, yet it is equally comforting when enjoyed hot. If you're looking for a gentle morning pick-me-up or a relaxing afternoon drink, this tea is a wonderful choice. Ingredients

  • 1 tea bag (Earl Grey, lemon tea, or your favorite tea - these are my two favorite)
  • ½ cup rhubarb, chopped
  • 1 cup water
  • 1 teaspoon lemon juice (optional)
  • 1 tablespoon sugar, or to taste (optional)
  • Other add-ons (such as chia seeds, gojiberry, red dates, mint...)

How to Make Rhubarb Tea

Step 1: Simmer the Rhubarb

Place the rhubarb, water, and sugar in a small saucepan.

Bring to a gentle simmer and cook until the rhubarb softens and releases its beautiful pink-red color into the water. This usually takes about 10 to 15 minutes.

Step 2: Steep the Tea

Turn off the heat and strain the liquid to remove the rhubarb pieces. While it's still hot (90-95oC), add the tea bag.

Let the tea steep in the hot rhubarb infusion for about 5 minutes. The tea will absorb the fruity flavor while adding its own aroma and depth.

Step 3: Strain and Finish

Remove the tea bag. Stir in the lemon juice if using. Taste and add more sugar if desired.

Step 4: Serve and Enjoy

Pour into your favorite mug and enjoy warm, or let it cool and serve over ice for a refreshing summer drink.

Garnish with a slice of lemon, fresh mint leaves, or a few pieces of rhubarb for a beautiful presentation.

Tips for the Best Rhubarb Tea

  • Earl Grey adds a lovely citrusy aroma that complements rhubarb perfectly.
  • Lemon tea creates an even brighter, more refreshing flavor.
  • For an iced version, chill the tea completely before serving over ice.
  • Adjust the sugar to suit your taste. Rhubarb can be quite tart, so some people may prefer a little extra sweetness.
  • Fresh rhubarb gives the best flavor, but frozen rhubarb works well too.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does rhubarb tea taste like?

Rhubarb tea has a bright, tangy flavor with gentle fruity notes. When combined with black tea, it becomes refreshing, aromatic, and lightly sweet.

Can I make rhubarb tea without sugar?

Yes. The sugar is completely optional. You can leave it out or replace it with honey, maple syrup, or your preferred sweetener.

Can I drink rhubarb tea cold?

Absolutely. This tea is delicious as an iced drink and is especially refreshing during summer.
